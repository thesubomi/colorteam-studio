Talk to us about Max

Already have brand guidelines?

You don't pay for a Brand Foundation. Instead, you pay a one-time Studio Setup fee: $330 on Core, $490 on Growth, $830 on Max. This covers a Colorteam designer reading your playbook, building your Studio templates from it, connecting your channels and checking the first batch of posts. After that, you pay only the monthly subscription.

Example: an early-stage startup with its own guidelines joining Core pays $330 once, then $149 a month.

Why no free plan?

Free plans show you what the AI can do. We'd rather show you what your brand looks like when someone with taste sets it up. Try Core for 14 days at the monthly rate. Move to annual and the Foundation fee is waived.
