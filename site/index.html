Every AI startup looks the same. Most of them have stopped posting.

The differentiation problem

You built a product in a weekend. Your website shipped with the same fonts, the same gradients and the same layout as the last fifty startups that used the same tool. Nobody can tell you apart.

The distribution problem

And if you did get a brand together, the posting never stuck. Every post means deciding what to say, opening a design tool, writing captions for four channels and finding the time to schedule it. So the feed goes quiet.

Differentiation gets you noticed. Distribution keeps you there. Most founders have neither.

Differentiation and distribution, in one system. Studio gives your brand a point of view: a design system built by Colorteam that makes everything you publish recognisably yours. Then it turns that system into output: posts, carousels, videos and landing pages, designed, captioned and scheduled without you opening a design tool.

It is not a template library. It is your brand, encoded, with a designer's eye on the way out.

Questions? We have answers.

Is this a design tool or a scheduler?

Neither on its own. Studio decides what to post, designs it to your brand, writes the copy and schedules it. The scheduler is a connector inside it.

What if I don't like the brand direction?

You see three directions before anything is built and you choose one. After it's locked, Studio keeps you consistent on purpose. If you want a new direction later, you can run a new Foundation.

How much of my time does it take?

About twenty minutes a week on Core to review and approve. Less on Growth. On Max you can hand approvals to your reviewer entirely.

Can I edit what Studio makes?

Yes. Every asset and every caption is editable before it ships.

Do I need to talk to a human to start?

Core and Growth start from the pricing page. Max starts with a short call.
