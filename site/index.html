Questions? We have answers.

What exactly is Brand Studio?

A private, browser-based design studio set up around your brand. Colorteam builds your templates and guidelines into it, and your team uses it to create on-brand content that you approve before it goes out.

Do I need a brand already?

No. If you don't have a brand system yet, Colorteam designs one first and then builds it into your Studio.

Who designs the templates?

Colorteam's designers. Each template is built from your brand, so your team can change the words and photos without breaking the design. Need a format we haven't built yet? Ask us.

Can my whole team use it?

Yes. You invite people by email, and you decide who can approve work.

What can I export?

PNG images for social media and PDF for documents such as proposals.

How much does it cost?

A one-off setup fee to build your Studio, then a monthly plan. See the pricing above, or book a demo and we'll talk it through.
