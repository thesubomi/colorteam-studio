1. Who we are

Colorteam Olasubomi Archibong, NIP PL8181729104, Dereniowa 9/20, 02-776 Warsaw, Poland ("Colorteam", "we") is the data controller for personal data processed through studio.colorteam.io and app.colorteam.io ("Studio"). Contact: subomi@colorteam.io.

2. What we collect

Account data: name, work email, company name, password hash, billing details handled by our payment processor.

Brand data: brand guidelines, logos, images, website links and other material you upload so Studio can produce content for you.

Connected services: when you connect a scheduling, analytics or video service, we receive the access tokens and data those services share with us, limited to what Studio needs to publish and report.

Usage data: pages visited, features used, device and browser information, IP address, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

Communications: messages you send us through forms, email or the call booking page.

3. Why we process it

  • To provide Studio: build your design system, generate and schedule content, report performance. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To bill you. Legal basis: contract and legal obligation.
  • To improve Studio and keep it secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • To send product updates and marketing, where you've agreed. Legal basis: consent, withdrawable at any time.
  • To comply with law. Legal basis: legal obligation.

4. AI processing

Studio uses third-party AI services to generate content from your brand data. Your brand material is sent to those providers only to produce your output and is not used to train their models under the terms we hold with them.

5. Who we share it with

Processors acting on our instructions: hosting, payment, email, analytics, AI generation, and the connectors you choose to enable. We do not sell personal data. We share data with authorities only where required by law.

6. International transfers

Some processors are outside the European Economic Area. Where that happens we rely on adequacy decisions or Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

Account and brand data for as long as your account is active and 12 months after closure, unless you ask for earlier deletion. Billing records for the period required by Polish tax law. Usage data up to 24 months.

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent. Email subomi@colorteam.io. You can also complain to the Polish supervisory authority, the Urząd Ochrony Danych Osobowych (UODO).

9. Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the site and, with your consent, analytics cookies to understand how it is used. Manage preferences through the cookie banner or your browser.

10. Changes

We will post updates here and, for material changes, email account holders.
