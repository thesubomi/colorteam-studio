About

Built by designers who got tired of watching good products go unnoticed.

Studio comes from Colorteam, a design-led marketing agency that has spent nine years helping brands find their position in the market, reach the right audience and grow revenue through sharp go-to-market execution.

Why Studio exists

For nine years we watched the same thing happen. A founder builds something worth talking about. They get a brand made, or they don't. Then the posting starts, stalls, and stops, because every post costs an hour nobody has.

AI changed the cost of making content. It did nothing for the cost of making it good. Now every startup can ship a hundred posts a month, and they all look the same. So the founders we work with were asking for two things at once: make us recognisable, and make the posting stop being a job.

Studio is our answer. The taste comes from the agency. The volume comes from the system. The founder gets their evenings back.

How we work

  • Taste before tooling. We define the brand first, because everything else is built on it.
  • Consistency over novelty. Recognisable beats clever. We lock directions in on purpose.
  • A human looks at the work. The AI handles the volume. A designer handles the judgment.
  • Content should end in a sale. Every campaign gets a landing page and a tracked link, because likes don't pay for the product.

The Agency

Studio is the product. Colorteam is the agency behind it. If you need a full rebrand, a campaign, or a marketing partner beyond what Studio does, that's us too.
