Step 1: Upload your brand guidelines

If you have them

Drop in your playbook, logo files and any assets you're proud of. Studio reads it and builds your templates from it. A designer checks the first batch so nothing drifts. You're at step two in days.

If you don't

This is where Colorteam comes in. You answer a short questionnaire about how you want your brand to look and sound, share your website link and any posts you like, and we come back with two directions in a mini mood board: logo, fonts, colours, sample posts. You pick one and lock it in. We then build your guidelines and your Studio on top of it.

Why we lock it in

Consistency is the whole point. Founders who redesign every month never get recognised. Once a direction is locked, Studio keeps you on it. Want a new direction later? Run a new Foundation.

See what's in a Brand Foundation

Step 2: We set up your Studio

Your design system goes in. Out come templates for every format Studio ships: stills, carousels, short-form video, talking-head frames, ads and a landing page. A built-in social media manager learns your voice and writes copy for every channel. Your calendar is created and ready.

You choose where Studio lives: on a subdomain of your own site, or hosted by us with a link you can redirect later. Either way, the assets are yours.

Step 3: Connect your channels

Connect your social accounts and your analytics through the connectors you already use. Scheduling runs through Metricool by default. GA4 connects so campaign traffic shows up on your site, not just in your feed. Max customers can add video generators and a digital twin for talking-head content.

See all integrations

Step 4: Post

Studio recommends two directions for a one-week campaign that drives traffic to your site, builds the landing page for it, and fills your calendar with three to four posts a week. You review the assets and copy, change what you want, and approve. Everything schedules at once or one by one.

Every post goes out with a trackable link, so you know where every visitor came from.

After launch

Each week Studio proposes the next batch. You approve in one sitting. On Growth, a Colorteam designer reviews every campaign before it reaches you. On Max, a named reviewer owns the account and you can stop logging in.

Time you spend: about twenty minutes a week on Core. Less on Growth. Close to zero on Max.
