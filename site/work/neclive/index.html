Case study

Powering Africa through one fingerprint

A scalable visual identity for NECLive 2025.

Creative economyVisual identityConference designLagos
At a glance

NECLive needed a 2025 identity that could carry its theme across dozens of touchpoints. We built it on one idea, the African fingerprint map, and scaled it to more than fifty assets.

Client
NECLive Conference
Event
28 November 2025, Landmark Centre, Lagos
Services
Visual identity, theme artwork, templates, digital assets
Design team
Olasubomi Archibong, Farouq Falade
50+assets in one visual language
1core idea, carried everywhere
3channels: digital, social, venue
300+attendees on the day
The challenge

Show power without the clichés

The 2025 theme, Powering Africa Through Creative Enterprise, had to speak to a pan-African audience, sit inside NECLive's existing brand and scale across sponsor-heavy assets.

  1. Cultural depth, no clichésNo spectacle, power symbols or overused Afrocentric motifs.
  2. One anchor that scalesA concept strong enough to hold every asset together.
  3. Built for volumeFlexible across digital, social and the venue, clear in sponsor-heavy layouts.
The idea

A fingerprint, shaped into a continent

A fingerprint stands for identity, authorship and human presence, so creativity reads as a people-powered force. Its lines flow outward into patterns and form the map of the continent. One line carries it: Africa's creative enterprise is powered by the people who shape it. Our identity is our power.

01 · Edition identity

A 2025 signature inside the existing brand

An edition lockup in NECLive's own type with a refined fingerprint map, and theme typography where the words themselves form the continent. The map stands alone as a hero graphic or weaves into any format.

02 · Pattern system

One pattern, many surfaces

The fingerprint became the base texture for banners, announcements, reels, story templates and speaker features, in three colourways: purple, pink and lime.

03 · Modular templates

Templates built for volume

A modular system kept sponsor-heavy layouts clear and balanced, so updates could ship fast: theme poster, host feature, media partner, creative partner.

Results

From one fingerprint to 50+ assets

On the day, the system carried from screens into the venue: entrance, booths, corridors and the main stage.
