Case study

Bringing Africa and the world to Ostrava

Brand, website and social campaign for AfrOstrava Festival.

FestivalBrand identityWebsiteSocial campaignCzech Republic
At a glance

AfrOstrava needed a brand, a home online and a year-round voice for a new multicultural festival. We built all three, and reached 171.1K viewers in the run-up to the 2026 edition.

The challenge

A new festival had to win over a new city

AfrOstrava had to introduce African music, dance, art and food to a Czech audience, earn the trust of partners, and sell tickets months before the gates opened.

  1. Credible from day oneA brand that partners, sponsors and the city could put their name to.
  2. Two audiences, one voiceCzech locals and the African and expat community, in Czech and English.
  3. Loud all yearMomentum for months before the festival, and between editions.
The brand

A brand as loud as the festival

A warped, hand-lettered AfrOstrava wordmark that moves like the music. Red, black and cream, with organic blob shapes and torn-paper edges across the site and socials. A poster system of sunburst frames and bold type, ready for every artist and announcement.

01 · Website

One home for tickets, partners and volunteers

Built to convert: ticket buttons in every section, partner and volunteer sign-ups, past events, FAQs and a newsletter. The venue story, a former ironworks turned cultural landmark, runs through it.

02 · Festival campaign

Every artist, a moment

Headliner reveals, artist cards, countdowns and ticket offers kept the festival in the feed for months, led by the CKay announcement.

03 · Year-round community

Always on, between festivals

Dance workshops, panel talks, playlists, heritage carousels, creator spotlights and culture stories kept the community growing all year: 184 posts between and around every event.

Results

368.5K views in the run-up to the festival

Instagram drove the community; Facebook drove the clicks. 15.3K profile visits and 10.9K link clicks across both platforms, measured September to June in Meta Business Suite.
