FAQ

Questions? We have answers.

If yours isn't here, ask us or book a call.

Getting started

Is this a design tool or a scheduler?

Neither on its own. Studio decides what to post, designs it to your brand, writes the copy and schedules it. The scheduler is a connector inside it.

Do I need a brand already?

No. If you don't have guidelines, a Brand Foundation comes first: Colorteam defines your brand and builds your Studio on top of it. If you do have guidelines, you skip the Foundation and pay a one-time Studio Setup fee instead.

How long until I'm live?

With existing guidelines, days. With a Brand Foundation, 7 working days on Core, 10 on Growth and 15 on Max, counted from the day we have everything we need from you.

Do I need to talk to a human to start?

Core and Growth start from the pricing page. Max starts with a short call.

Brand and design

What if I don't like the brand direction?

You see two directions before anything is built and you choose one. After it's locked, Studio keeps you consistent on purpose. If you want a new direction later, you can run a new Foundation.

Why do you lock the direction in?

Founders who redesign every month never get recognised. Consistency is what makes the system work, so once a direction is confirmed, Studio holds it.

I don't have a logo. Can I still start on Core?

Yes. Add the Starter Logo to Core for $490: one typographic wordmark, one round. If you want a full mark with an icon, that's included in Growth.

Who owns the brand assets?

You do, once the Foundation is paid in full. Logos, guidelines, templates and everything Studio makes for you are yours.

Using Studio

How much of my time does it take?

About twenty minutes a week on Core to review and approve. Less on Growth. On Max you can hand approvals to your reviewer entirely.

Can I edit what Studio makes?

Yes. Every asset and every caption is editable before it ships.

What does the human actually do at each tier?

On Core, nobody is in the loop; you approve. On Growth, a Colorteam designer reviews each campaign before it reaches you and sends a monthly performance summary. On Max, a named reviewer owns your account, joins a monthly call and runs a quarterly brand refresh.

Does it work for my channels?

LinkedIn, Instagram, X, TikTok, Facebook and YouTube Shorts, through Metricool and the connectors you choose. More on request.

How accurate is the performance benchmark?

It is a guide, not a prediction. The score is based on format, hook and platform norms. Once you're posting, your own analytics replace the benchmark.

Where does Studio live?

On a subdomain of your own site, or hosted by us. Either way, your assets are yours.

Billing

How much does it cost?

A one-time Brand Foundation ($990, $1,490 or $2,490) or Studio Setup fee ($330, $490 or $830 if you already have guidelines), then a monthly subscription of $149, $349 or $799.

Is there a free plan?

No. Free plans show you what the AI can do. We'd rather show you what your brand looks like when someone with taste sets it up. Try Core for 14 days at the monthly rate.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from your account. Service runs to the end of the paid period. Annual plans with the Foundation fee waived settle the Foundation pro rata if cancelled before twelve months.

Can I change tier later?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately; the Foundation difference is billed once. Downgrades apply from your next renewal.
