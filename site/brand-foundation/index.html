Brand Foundation

Taste first. Everything else is built on it.

A Brand Foundation is the one-time design work Colorteam does before your Studio goes live. It is the difference between content that looks generated and content that looks like you.

Why it comes first

Studio can produce a hundred posts a month. If the brand underneath is the same default every AI tool ships, that is a hundred posts that look like everyone else's. The Foundation sets the rules Studio will follow for as long as you use it: what your logo is, how your type behaves, which colours carry which message, what your voice sounds like. Get that right once and every post after it is on-brand by default.

How it runs

  1. Questionnaire. Twenty minutes on how you want to look and sound. Share your website and any posts you like.
  2. Two directions. A mini mood board for each: logo, fonts, colours, sample posts. Delivered within three working days.
  3. Pick and lock. You choose one. We ask you to confirm, because after this point we build.
  4. Build. Guidelines, templates and, on Max, your website. Refinement rounds as listed above.
  5. Handover. Your Foundation goes into Studio. Setup begins the same day.
